# qdCFCs

ColdFusion Components from **QuantumDelta**, the B2B SaaS company I founded in
Tulsa, Oklahoma in December 2001 and ran for eleven years.

These are the service-layer components that sat behind [QDCMS](https://github.com/the1drewharris/qdcms),
the CMS the business ran on. Released for the same reason that one was: when I
wound QuantumDelta down in 2012, hundreds of businesses were running their
day-to-day operations on this software, and I wanted them to have somewhere to
go and somebody who could keep it running.

Archive, not a maintained project. No support and no releases. Written between
2001 and 2012 — read it as history, or for parts.
